# Preset layout sketches (provisional)

Tile sketches of the four presets from `docs/agents/PRESETS_PLAN.md` on the 16 × 16 tile board (1 tile = 4 world units; columns 0–F left→right = west→east, rows 0–F top→bottom = north→south). Every piece, ramp and approach is inside the board. Footprints come from the measured City Kit models (`assets/3d/ASSETS.md`). These assume round 5 answers Q1 (divided road = wide) and Q2 (warning sign = yield); exact tiles are confirmed when each preset is built.

Legend: `.` grass (free space) · `-` / `|` road, one lane each way · `=` divided road (two one-way two-lane carriageways, one per row) · `#` signal-controlled junction · `X` stop-controlled junction · `T` / `+` junction or corner · `R` roundabout (3 × 3) · `S` signal-controlled crossing · `z` pedestrian crossing · `d` driveway · `<` road split (two lanes → one) · `/` `\` ramp up / down · `B` bridge (flyover deck) · `o` **open connection point** for players. Road ends at the board edge are entry/exit points.
